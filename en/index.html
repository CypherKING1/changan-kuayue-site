Changan Kuayue V7 at a Glance

The Changan Kuayue V7 EV is a fully electric passenger-and-cargo vehicle built for efficient work and versatile mobility. Developed to LGV (Light Goods Vehicle) standards with a 3135mm long wheelbase and a large body, it offers class-leading loading space. A flexible fold-and-slide 7-seat layout, a powerful high-output electric drivetrain and refined, stable handling let you enjoy private-car comfort while easily handling high-volume cargo — the all-round partner for your business and life.

Specifications

Flexible 7-seat layout with class-leading cargo space

A clear look at the refined cabin and practical space structure. It comfortably seats 7 while offering the largest private loading space in its class — passenger transport and cargo hauling in one, truly one vehicle for many uses.

Wide body and long wheelbase, built to commercial standards

The vehicle measures 4800 × 1920 × 1680 mm in length, width, and height, with an extra-long wheelbase of 3135 mm. The entire vehicle is designed in strict accordance with light commercial vehicle compliance standards, featuring a robust and sturdy body and extremely high space utilization.

Transformable boot, up to 2200mm depth

Base boot dimensions reach 1500 × 1200 × 1300 mm. The third-row seats fold and slide forward, instantly extending depth to 2200 mm — customise loading combinations on demand and carry oversized items with ease.

Powerful electric drive, smooth and comfortable handling

A high-output motor delivers strong, responsive acceleration. Paired with a finely tuned chassis and suspension, it stays remarkably comfortable and stable even through corners and challenging road conditions.

Big space and comfort in one

V7 EV Cabin

The Changan Kuayue V7 EV blends a human-centric driving experience with a comfortable passenger cabin. A wrap-around cockpit paired with a smart connected display makes operation effortless and efficient, while the spacious 7-seat cabin and independent rear air conditioning deliver above-class ride quality — whether for team travel or dual passenger-and-cargo use.

Wrap-around panoramic cockpit, efficient and human-centric

An open layout with an expansive field of view and controls within easy reach. It balances ergonomics with space efficiency, making daily driving and cargo work easier and less tiring.

Spacious front driving space

Front ventilated seats. A clean, well-organised centre console offers ample head and leg room, and comfortable enveloping seats help reduce fatigue on long journeys.

HD digital cluster, driving info at a glance

An integrated digital instrument display clearly shows battery, range, speed and other core driving data, helping the driver stay precisely aware of the vehicle status at all times.

9-inch multimedia display, smart and seamless connectivity

An integrated 9-inch touchscreen supports wireless Apple CarPlay, radio, media playback, Bluetooth calling, navigation and a rear-view camera — enhancing convenience and safety on every trip.

Passenger-car column shifter, freeing up front space

A convenient column-mounted shifter feels natural to operate and frees up the walkway between the front seats, making the cockpit feel more open.

Independent rear A/C, cool comfort throughout

Equipped with a dedicated independent rear air-conditioning unit and vents, cabin temperature can be adjusted separately to keep every passenger comfortable.

Independent twin second row, easier movement

The second row uses a left-and-right independent twin-seat layout with a relaxed seating posture and a central walkway, letting third-row passengers get in and out with ease.

Wide-body 3-seat third row, a genuinely usable 7-seater

Built on the 1920mm wide body, the third row comfortably seats three adults — no more cramped quarters, fully supporting multi-passenger travel needs.

Space without limits, swallowing all kinds of bulky items

Several large suitcases are just the tip of the iceberg of its huge capacity. The neat, open boot not only easily absorbs the whole crew’s travel gear, but also freely holds bulky cargo, work equipment and extra-long tools — however you load it, it never feels restricted.
